## What is Catena?

Catena provides service chaining capabilities on Cisco Nexus 9000 switches, enabling:

- Dynamic insertion of network services
- Traffic steering through service nodes
- Integration with physical and virtual appliances
- Health monitoring and failover
- Automated service insertion
